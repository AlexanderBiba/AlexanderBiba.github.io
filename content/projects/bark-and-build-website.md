This project was built to answer one question every construction business owner asks:  
**“Will this website bring me more jobs?”**

For **Bark & Build**, I designed and developed a conversion-first website focused on **turning local traffic into real leads**. The site is structured around how homeowners actually search for contractors—clear services, strong local SEO, and trust signals above the fold.

The **video hero section** immediately establishes credibility. A clean **project portfolio** showcases real work (not stock photos). The **SEO-driven blog** targets high-intent local keywords to drive consistent organic traffic over time.

The key differentiator is the **AI Project Estimator**. Visitors describe their project, receive an instant rough estimate, and submit their contact info—**pre-qualifying leads before the first call** and saving the contractor time.

### Core features
- Clear service breakdown with **dedicated pages for each service**  
- **About Us** page focused on trust and credibility  
- Multiple **contact forms** optimized for conversions  
- Sleek **customer testimonials** for strong social proof  
- **Portfolio system** where owners can upload and manage project images  
- **Blog system** with rich posts and image support  
- Fully customizable **admin panel** — no developer required:
  - Create and edit blog posts and portfolio entries  
  - Update page titles, copy, CTAs, and SEO content  
  - Manage social media links and site-wide settings  
  - Configure key sections without contacting a developer
