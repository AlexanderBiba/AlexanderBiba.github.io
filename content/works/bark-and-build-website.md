A website for Bark & Build Builders, a residential construction company in the Bay Area that does ADUs, remodels and additions.

I built it for them with one goal, get them more jobs from local search.

So the site is laid out around how homeowners actually look for a contractor. Every service has its own page, there's a portfolio of real projects and testimonials, and there's a contact form wherever someone might be ready to reach out. There's also a blog aimed at local searches from people who are ready to hire.

The one unusual piece is an AI estimator. Visitors describe their project, get a rough number back and leave their contact details, so the leads that come in are already somewhat qualified. The owners can edit everything through a CMS, so they can add projects, write posts and change copy without calling me.

It's built with Next.js, TypeScript and Tailwind, with Sanity for content, Supabase for form submissions and media, and Resend for email notifications. It runs on Vercel.
